How it works

The book is the board. The phone is everything that hides, moves or keeps a secret.

Open the book

Turn to a blank game page and give everyone a pencil. You draw and write right on the page.

Scan the code

Each game page has a code. Your phone opens the right game, and walks you through your first turns.

Play, then show off

At the end, make a winner's card with the date, the scores and a photo if you like.

Ages 8 and up1 to 4 playersNo app to installNo accountNothing is stored
